Ever since high school, I've held teaching & tutoring positions in mathematics and data science, always with the same goal in mind: to make STEM topics easy, engaging, and fun. I strongly believe everyone has the capabilities to excel in STEM, and just need the resources and teaching style that adhears to them the best. Hence, I have engaged in these positions with the hopes of steering students towards better understanding and appreciating these subjects.
