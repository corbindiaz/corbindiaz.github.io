# Hello! 👋

I am a junior majoring in **Data Science** and **Mathematics** with a minor in Cognitive Science at **Northwestern University**. I am passionate for applying mathematics and statistics to help make a positive impact on the world through data analysis and machine learning, and have a major interest in data visualization, both in its implementation and in making it more effective. I hold a strong skillset in both **Python** and **R**, and in the past, I've appplied my visualization skills through weather data interactives and data dashboards for Chicagoland education non-profit Project SYNCERE. Having served as a teaching assistant in both the Data Science and Math departments, I enjoy helping a variety of students in tutoring roles in making STEM topics more accessible and easy to understand. 

I am also interested in topics related to neuroscience, notably in cognitive models and mental health, and also enjoy music, film editing, and linguistics. Currently, I am researching methods in classifying mysterious infrasound signals through the Department of Earth, Environmental and Planetary Science at Northwestern.
